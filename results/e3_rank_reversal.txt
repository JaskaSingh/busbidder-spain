E3 Rank reversal: leave each accepted offer out and rank the rest again

Strategy: anchored
  budget     8 offers: 0 of 8 removals change the order of the rest, 0 change the winner
  business   5 offers: 0 of 5 removals change the order of the rest, 0 change the winner
  flexible  11 offers: 0 of 11 removals change the order of the rest, 0 change the winner

Strategy: minmax
  budget     8 offers: 1 of 8 removals change the order of the rest, 1 change the winner
  business   5 offers: 1 of 5 removals change the order of the rest, 0 change the winner
  flexible  11 offers: 0 of 11 removals change the order of the rest, 0 change the winner
